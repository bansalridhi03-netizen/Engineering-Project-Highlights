# Engineering Project Highlights

## Overview

This repository presents a selection of my previous engineering, robotics, and research projects, highlighting my experience in mechanical design, automation, computer vision, robotic systems, and emerging engineering technologies.

## Featured Projects

### 1. Robotics and Automation
- **Soft Modular Cellbots:** Design, simulation, fabrication, and experimental investigation of self-reconfigurable soft robotic systems.
- **Romi Robot Path Following:** Autonomous black-line following, end-of-path detection, and shortest-path return to the starting position.
- **Robot Path Planning and Digital Twins:** Robot simulation, navigation, and collision avoidance using ROS and Gazebo.

### 2. Industrial and Research Projects
- **NUVision / NHS:** Computer vision and image-processing project work.
- **Toshiba:** Robotic storage and retrieval concepts.
- **Thales Alenia Space:** Robot isolator design and workspace optimisation.

### 3. Mechanical Design and Manufacturing
- CAD modelling and mechanical component design.
- CNC manufacturing and engineering prototyping.

### 4. Emerging Engineering Concepts
- Artificial muscle technologies and actuation methods.
- Energy harvesting concepts and applications.
