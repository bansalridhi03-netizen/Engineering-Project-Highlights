# Engineering Project Highlights — Portfolio Presentation

## Presentation Overview

This presentation showcases a selection of my previous engineering, robotics, and research projects, highlighting my experience across mechanical design, automation, computer vision, robotic systems, and emerging engineering technologies.

## Featured Project Areas

### 1. Robotics and Automation
- Soft Modular Cellbots: Design, simulation, fabrication, and experimental testing of self-reconfigurable soft robots.
- Romi Robot Path Following: Autonomous black-line following and shortest-path return navigation.
- Robot Path Planning and Digital Twins: Robot simulation, path planning, and collision avoidance using ROS and Gazebo.

### 2. Industrial and Research Projects
- NUVision / NHS: Computer vision and image-processing projects.
- Toshiba: Robotic storage and retrieval concepts.
- Thales Alenia Space: Robot isolator design and workspace optimisation.

### 3. Mechanical Design and Manufacturing
- Mechanical CAD modelling and component design.
- CNC manufacturing and engineering prototyping.

### 4. Emerging Engineering Concepts
- Artificial muscle technologies and actuation methods.
- Energy harvesting concepts and applications.
